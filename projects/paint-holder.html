Paint Robot Nozzle Holder

A robotic paint gun used to coat track idlers tended to drift slowly away from its intended position. I designed a holder to keep the nozzle rigid and holding its aim.

  • CAD
  • Design for rigidity
  • Process repeatability
Application
Robotic paint gun coating track idlers
Issue
Nozzle gradually moving off its intended position
My role
Design and CAD modeling of the holder
Step 01

Problem

The paint nozzle position could drift over time, so its aim did not stay where it was set.

Step 02

Design

A custom holder that grips the nozzle and constrains it rigidly.

Step 03

Goal

Reduce movement and improve positioning repeatability in an existing automated process.

Overview

A small part fixing a process problem

Track idlers are wheels used in tracked equipment such as bulldozers, and they were painted by a robot. The gun mounted on that robot had a tendency to move slowly out of position, which meant the nozzle was no longer pointed where the program assumed it was.

The fix was mechanical rather than programmatic. I designed a nozzle holder to keep the nozzle steady, improve rigidity at the mount, reduce that gradual movement, and maintain consistent aim through the cycle.

This is a small project, but it is the kind that comes up constantly in a production environment: an automated process that works in principle is being undermined by one component that will not stay where it is put.
