About

Close to the hardware, across disciplines

I'm an Aerospace Engineering student at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign who enjoys working close to the hardware and carrying ideas from design into the real world. My experience has ranged from aircraft CAD and flight testing to robotics, automation, machining, CAD/CAM, controls, manufacturing, and hands-on troubleshooting.

I'm especially interested in aerospace and space systems, but what I like most is engineering work that crosses disciplines and lets me understand, build, test, and improve a full system rather than staying confined to one narrow task.

Alongside coursework I've been involved with Liquid Rocketry at Illinois, including the Engine subgroup, and with ISS SpaceShot.
